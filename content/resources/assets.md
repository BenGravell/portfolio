## Images, Illustrations & Icons

- [unDraw](https://undraw.co/) - open-source illustrations, free to use without attribution ([license](https://undraw.co/license))
- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/) - high-resolution photography, free to use without attribution ([license](https://unsplash.com/license))
- [Freepik](https://www.freepik.com/) - large library of vectors, photos, and icons; many free with attribution — check the license per asset
- [Flaticon](https://www.flaticon.com/) - extensive icon library; many free with attribution — check the license per asset

## Audio

- [YouTube Audio Library](https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary) - royalty-free music and sound effects; accessed within [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/) — check the license per track
